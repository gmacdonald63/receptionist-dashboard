HOW THESE NUMBERS ARE WORKED OUT

A size like 265/65R18 reads as: 265 mm across the tread, a sidewall 65% of that width tall, wrapped around an 18 inch wheel. So sidewall = 265 × 0.65 = 172.3 mm, and overall diameter = 18" + two sidewalls = 31.6". Revolutions per mile = 63,360 ÷ circumference in inches.

Percentages are taken from the rounded figures shown in the table, so the column always adds up the way you see it. The ±3% window is the usual industry limit for a plus-size or minus-size fitment — past that, the speedometer, odometer, ABS and traction control are all working from the wrong rolling distance. These are nominal specs from the size code; a real tire's molded dimensions vary a little by brand and tread pattern, and this doesn't check whether the tire clears your fenders or suspension.
